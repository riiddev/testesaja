# Vocabulary data attribution

## Oxford headword list

The source headwords are the user-provided simplified and deduplicated version of **The Oxford 3000 (American English)**. Oxford's published list is available at:

https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/external/pdf/wordlists/oxford-3000-5000/American_Oxford_3000.pdf

Oxford Learner's Dictionaries / Oxford University Press are the source of the list and CEFR classification. SinauuEnglish is an independent learning project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Oxford University Press. The supplied simplified list contains 2,971 unique headwords; it omits the POS/CEFR distinctions and multiple same-spelling entries shown in the PDF.

## English–Indonesian dictionary enrichment

Glosses, English sense definitions, IPA where present, and usage examples are enriched from the KOReader English–Indonesian dictionary:

- Project and downloads: https://github.com/DanielGregorini/koreader-dicts
- Dictionary entry: English → Indonesian (en-id), generated 2026-09-21.
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC-BY-SA-4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The dictionary compilation lists these source datasets:
- omw-id: WordNet Bahasa, via Open Multilingual Wordnet 1.4.
- pwn30: Princeton University WordNet 3.0.
- wikt-en-id: English Wiktionary via kaikki.org (wiktextract).

## Translation status

Indonesian explanations and example-sentence translations generated from English definitions/examples are machine-generated draft translations and have not been individually human-reviewed. The app distinguishes headword imports from entries that have enough real content to study, and does not claim that every imported entry is pedagogically complete.
